## (Upcoming) Spring 2027: Special Topics in Aerospace Engineering - Introduction to Composite Materials (AENG 4930/5930)
This course will provide students with a foundational knowledge and analytical tools required to design and evaluate composite structures for engineering applications. Students will learn composite material selection, manufacturing techniques, stress analysis using laminated plate theories and appropriate failure criteria, and the behavior of composites under static, cyclic, and long-term loading conditions, including fatigue and delamination damage. The course also introduces practical applications of composites in aerospace, biomedical, sports, and other advanced engineering fields.

## Fall 2026: Materials Science (MENGX 3510)

Materials Science is a multidisciplinary field requiring knowledge of chemistry, physics, and mechanics. In this first course we examine effect of chemistry on molecular structure and physical and mechanical properties of materials, and we examine methods of controlling those properties.
