nanoscale:
  - image_path: /assets/images/Epon-862.jpg
    alt: "Molecular model of an epoxy polymer"
    title: "Designing Better Composites, Atom by Atom"
    excerpt: "**The question:** How do tiny additives like carbon nanotubes and graphene make a polymer stronger, tougher, or more conductive, and how do we choose the best recipe without endless trial and error?<br><br>**How we tackle it:** Molecular dynamics simulations that capture what happens at the interface between the additive and the polymer, then connect those atomic-scale details to bulk material properties.<br><br>**Your role:** Build molecular models, run simulations on SLU's high-performance computing cluster, and analyze how structure controls properties."

damage:
  - image_path: /assets/images/CT-Damage.png
    alt: "X-ray CT scan showing internal damage in a composite"
    title: "Seeing Hidden Damage"
    excerpt: "**The question:** An impact can leave a carbon-fiber part looking fine on the outside while cracking it inside. How do we find and measure damage we can't see?<br><br>**How we tackle it:** Ultrasonic testing, including phased-array scanning, and X-ray computed tomography, combined with automated and AI-assisted analysis to turn scans into numbers engineers can trust.<br><br>**Your role:** Manufacture and impact composite panels, scan them, and write analysis code that quantifies the damage and links it to remaining strength."

structures:
  - image_path: /assets/images/FEM.png
    alt: "Finite element model of a composite structure"
    title: "Testing and Modeling Composite Structures"
    excerpt: "**The question:** How do composite structures deform and fail under real loads, and can we predict it before it happens?<br><br>**How we tackle it:** Mechanical testing to ASTM standards on our 100 kN universal test frame with 3D digital image correlation to map strain across the whole specimen, paired with finite element models in tools like ABAQUS and ANSYS.<br><br>**Your role:** Make and test specimens, capture full-field strain data, and build models that match what you see in the lab."

manufacturing:
  - image_path: /assets/images/Lopez.jpg
    alt: "Friction stir additive manufacturing"
    title: "Advanced and In-Space Manufacturing"
    excerpt: "**The question:** Can we build strong metal parts layer by layer without melting them, even from lunar regolith or recycled space debris?<br><br>**How we tackle it:** Solid-state friction stir additive manufacturing, followed by mechanical testing and non-destructive inspection of what we build.<br><br>**Your role:** Help process and characterize printed metal and metal-matrix composite parts, and study how processing controls their properties."
