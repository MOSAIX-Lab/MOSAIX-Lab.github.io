## One framework, <em>many scales.</em>

<p class="lead">Advanced materials for aerospace, energy and biomedical applications face extreme environments: impact, thermal extremes and corrosive conditions.</p>

Making these materials more resilient is essential to the safety, reliability and longevity of engineered systems. Our work integrates materials science, multiscale computational mechanics and rigorous experimental validation through advanced non-destructive evaluation (NDE), so that we can intentionally design multifunctional structural materials with long-term resilience and sustainability.

At the **nanoscale**, molecular dynamics simulations reveal how inclusions such as carbon nanotubes and graphene change a polymer's mechanical, thermal and multifunctional properties. At the **macroscale**, we characterize damage and degradation in composites subjected to impact and lightning strike, and use phased array ultrasonic testing and X-ray computed tomography to validate our damage models.
